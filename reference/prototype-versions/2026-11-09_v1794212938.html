We call them partners because they work with us, but they are separate businesses: this is not a legal partnership, joint venture or agency. We show their prices and availability, and take bookings and payments for them. We are not a party to the contract for the work or the sale. Each listing and booking shows the business you are dealing with.

Who is responsible for the work or vehicle

The partner or dealer is responsible for the work they carry out, the parts and goods they supply, the vehicles they sell, and any warranty or guarantee they give. If something goes wrong, your claim is against them. We will help you contact them and, if needed, give you their full business details.

Your statutory rights

Services must be carried out with reasonable care and skill, and goods and vehicles must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described. These rights come from the Consumer Rights Act 2015 and cannot be removed by these terms. You use them against the partner or dealer.

Checks we carry out

We check each partner’s photo ID, trade insurance and public liability insurance before they can take bookings, and again when documents expire. These checks help keep the marketplace safe, but they are not a guarantee of the quality of any work.

Prices, payment and our booking fee

Prices are set by each partner. When you pay through Motoring Central, we collect the payment on the partner’s behalf, and paying us counts as paying them. Our booking fee [currently £2.50 per booking, launch offer] is shown before you book. If the partner cancels, we refund the booking fee.

Cancelling a booking

You can cancel free of charge up to [24 hours] before the booked time. If you cancel later, the partner may charge a cancellation fee shown at the time of booking. Recovery jobs that are already under way cannot be cancelled free of charge.

Problems and complaints

Contact the partner or dealer first, using the details in your booking or the advert. If you cannot resolve it, contact us at [email] and we will try to help both sides reach an agreement. This does not affect your right to take the matter further.

Our responsibility to you

We are responsible for running the website and app with reasonable care and skill, and for the accuracy of information we write ourselves. We are not responsible for the work, parts, goods or vehicles supplied by partners or dealers. Nothing in these terms limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or for anything else that cannot be limited by law.

Reviews

Reviews must be honest and about a real booking or purchase. We may remove reviews that are false, offensive or unlawful.

Law

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales. If you live in Scotland or Northern Ireland, you can also bring proceedings in your local courts.

2. Garages, mobile mechanics and auto electricians

These terms apply when you book a service, repair, diagnostic or electrical job with a garage, mobile mechanic or auto electrician. Wherever this section says “garage or mechanic”, it includes auto electricians.

Your contract is with the garage or mechanic

The garage or mechanic named in your booking carries out the work and is responsible for it.

Warranty

The warranty on the workmanship and on any parts fitted is given by the garage or mechanic, not by Motoring Central. They must show their warranty terms before you book. Warranty claims are made directly to them. Parts carry the manufacturer’s warranty, which the garage or mechanic will deal with for you.

Extra work

If the garage or mechanic finds more work is needed, they must explain it and get your agreement and a price before doing it. You do not have to pay for extra work you did not agree to.

Mobile work

For mobile bookings you must make sure the vehicle is somewhere it is safe and legal to work on, and that you have permission to have work done there. If the job cannot be done safely, the mechanic may rearrange it.

Damage

The garage or mechanic is responsible for any damage they cause to your vehicle or property while working on it, and must hold insurance to cover this.

3. MOT tests

MOT tests are carried out by DVSA-authorised test centres listed on Motoring Central. The test result is the test centre’s decision. If you disagree with a result, raise it with the test centre first; you can also appeal to the DVSA. Any repairs after the test are a separate job with the garage, with its own price and warranty from that garage.

4. Tyre fitting

The tyre fitter named in your booking supplies and fits the tyres and is responsible for the fitting work and its warranty. Tyres carry the manufacturer’s warranty, which the fitter will deal with for you. Puncture repairs are only carried out where the tyre can be safely repaired; if it cannot, the fitter will tell you and offer a replacement. Follow the fitter’s advice on rechecking wheel nuts after fitting.

5. Valeting

The valeter named in your booking carries out the valet and is responsible for their work and any damage they cause. Please remove valuables before the valet. Report any problem or damage to the valeter within [48 hours], with photos if you can, so they can put it right.

6. Bodywork and alloy wheels

Your contract is with the repairer

The body shop, smart repairer or wheel specialist you choose carries out the work and is responsible for it, including any damage they cause while your vehicle is in their care.

Quotes from photos

Bodywork quotes are based on the photos and details you send. If the repairer finds more damage once they see the vehicle, they must explain it and agree a new price with you before doing extra work. You do not have to pay for work you did not agree to.

Warranty

The warranty on the repair, paintwork and alloy finish is given by the repairer, not by Motoring Central. They must show their warranty terms before you book. Paint is matched as closely as possible, but small differences in shade can occur on older or faded paint.

Insurance claims

If you are claiming on insurance, the claim is between you and your insurer. Check with your insurer before the repair. “Insurance approved” means the repairer has told us, and we have checked, that they are on at least one insurer or accident management panel. It does not mean your own insurer will accept them.

Alloy wheels

Wheels with cracks, buckles or corrosion may need straightening or may not be safe to refurbish. The specialist will tell you before starting. Check wheel nuts are tight after the work, as advised by the specialist.

7. Recovery, breakdown and mobile EV charging

For mobile EV charging, the charging company named in your booking is responsible for connecting and charging your vehicle safely with suitable, tested equipment. The price covers the call-out and the charge you chose. Follow the operator’s safety instructions at the roadside.

The recovery company named in your booking carries out the recovery and is responsible for your vehicle from when they load it until they deliver it. They must hold insurance for vehicles in their care. Prices are based on the details you give; if the job turns out to be different (for example a longer distance or extra equipment), the company must tell you the new price before carrying on. Please remove valuables, and always follow the driver’s safety instructions at the roadside.

The same applies when you book a recovery partner to deliver a car or van you have bought. Delivery is a separate booking with the recovery partner. Make sure the vehicle is paid for and the seller has agreed the collection before the delivery day.

8. Buying a car or van

Your contract is with the dealer

Cars and vans on Motoring Central are advertised and sold by independent dealers. Any sale, deposit, part exchange, finance or delivery is agreed between you and the dealer. Motoring Central does not own, inspect or sell the vehicles.

Liability and warranty stay with the dealer

The dealer is responsible for the advert being accurate, for the vehicle’s condition, history and roadworthiness, for making sure they have the right to sell it, and for any warranty they offer. Any claim about the vehicle is made against the dealer.

Your rights

When you buy from a dealer, the vehicle must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described. If it is faulty you may have the right to reject it within 30 days, or to a repair or replacement after that. If you buy without visiting the dealer, you may also have cancellation rights. These rights are against the dealer. Vans bought for business use have fewer consumer rights.

Before you buy

We recommend you view and test drive the vehicle, check the V5C and service history, and run a vehicle history check before paying a deposit.

11. Trade sales (trade to trade, sold as seen)

Who can use it

The Trade section is only for logged-in partners and dealers buying and selling as businesses. Private buyers cannot use it.

Sold as seen, no warranty

Every vehicle in the Trade section is sold as seen, trade to trade, with no warranty from the seller or from Motoring Central. Business buyers do not have the consumer rights that private buyers have. The buyer must inspect the vehicle and rely on their own checks.

Honest descriptions

Sellers must describe vehicles accurately, list all known faults, and have the right to sell them. A vehicle sold as seen must still match its description.

Prices and offers

Sellers either set a minimum price, which a buyer can accept straight away, or invite offers with a hidden minimum. Offers below the minimum are declined automatically. When a seller accepts an offer or a buyer buys at the minimum price, the two businesses agree payment and collection directly. Motoring Central is not a party to the sale.
