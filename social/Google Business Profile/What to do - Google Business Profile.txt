GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE - check before you apply

Google Business Profile is for businesses that meet their customers in person,
at their own premises or by visiting them. An online-only business usually does
not qualify, and Google can suspend a profile that does not meet its rules.

The Motoring Central is an online platform: the garages, dealers and recovery
companies meet the customers, not us. So it probably does not qualify.

What to do instead:
- Encourage every partner on the network to keep their own Google Business Profile
  up to date. That is where local customers find them.
- If the business later has premises where customers visit, apply then.
