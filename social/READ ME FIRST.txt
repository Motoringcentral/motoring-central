THE MOTORING CENTRAL - SOCIAL MEDIA KIT

One folder per platform. Each folder has the images for that platform, already
named and sized, and a "What to do" note with the steps and the text to paste in.

Before you start:
1. Add a free alias social@motoringcentral.co.uk in Google Admin and use it for every account.
2. Use the same username everywhere: themotoringcentral (or motoringcentraluk if taken).
3. Turn on two-step login on every account.

Order: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, then X (optional).
Google Business Profile: read the note in its folder first.

Hold off on paid adverts aimed at drivers until the site is live at
motoringcentral.co.uk. Until then, post partner recruitment only.

The folder "_originals (do not upload)" holds the master images. Ignore it.
