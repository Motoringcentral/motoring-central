Which MOT do I need, and what does it cost?

It depends on your vehicle. Most cars, motorhomes, taxis and vans up to 3,000kg are class 4. Motorcycles are class 1 or 2, three-wheelers up to 450kg are class 3, minibuses with 13 or more seats are class 5, and larger vans from 3,000kg to 3,500kg are class 7. Vehicles with 9 or more seats may also need a seat belt installation check (class 4a or 5a).

Choose your MOT type when you book and we show each garage’s price. Garages set their own fees, up to the legal maximum for the class: for example £54.85 for a car and £29.65 for a motorcycle.

Can a mobile mechanic do an MOT?

No. An MOT must be done at an approved test station. Choose MOT, pick your type of MOT, and book at a partner test station.

I have lost my car keys or locked them in the car. Can you help?

Yes. Choose Auto electrician and locksmith. A vehicle locksmith can open the car without damage, cut and program a new key or fob, and wipe lost keys from the car’s memory. They will ask for proof that the vehicle is yours.

My electric car has run out of charge. What can you do?

Do you repair vans as well as cars?
